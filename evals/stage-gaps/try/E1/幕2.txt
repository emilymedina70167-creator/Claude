# E1 第 2 幕：进行 = be + doing。拨时间、换主语，点出一直没变的词；主语换成 The price of these homes 时自己挑 is / are；最后圈 Every cat 那句的谓语
sentence s = "Home prices are holding steady in most regions."
mark s.3-4 谓语
array tl = 过去 现在 将来
ptr 小人 tl.2
label wave tl.2 "〰 正在进行" side=above tone=note
dial t = 2 range=1-3 at=tl
say "这回只拨时间，看胶囊里哪个词一直没动？"
ask tap s "哪个词一直没动？" expect=s.4
学生: tap s.4
glow s.4 g4
rewrite s "The price of these homes ___ holding steady in most regions."
sentence c = "is are"
say "那前面那个 be 在管什么？这时候用哪个？先自己放。"
ask tap c "挑一块放进空格" expect=c.1
学生: tap c.2
rewrite s "The price of these homes are holding steady in most regions."
refer s.6 -> s.5
dim s.3-5
say "{s.6} 连到的是 {s.5}，可 of these homes 是修饰。"
ask tap s "主语的核心词是哪个？" expect=s.2
学生: tap s.2
rewrite s "Every cat with an owner, for instance, is running a small-scale study in operant conditioning."
remove c
say "进行 = be + doing，两个合起来才是谓语。圈出这一句的谓语。"
ask say "这一句的谓语是哪几个词？写出来。"
学生: say is
say "好。"
ask next "下一步" button="好"
