# E1 第 3 幕：完成 = 站在一个时间点回头看。时间轴用平面的 x 轴（原点是「现在」），小人用一根能拖的向量；sent 是过去的一个点
plane p range=-4..4
point p S = -2 0
label sent p.S "sent（寄来优惠券）" side=below tone=note
vec p me = -2 0 color=g4
label lamp p.me "小人站在这儿，灯往身后照" side=above tone=note
sentence s = "He had never gambled before the casino sent him a coupon."
mark s.2-4 谓语
say "拖这个小人，盯住胶囊——哪个词跟着它变？"
open p.me drag
goal g1 "把小人拖到「现在」" when="p.me = (0,0)"
@g1 rewrite s "He has never gambled before."
@g1 say "站到现在，{s.2} 换了。"
ask next "拖过三处点下面" button="拖好了"
学生: vec p me = 0 0
学生: vec p me = 3 0
学生: next
say "那 had 在说什么？"
ask say "had 在说什么？"
学生: say 已经在那之前了
rewrite s "By 1830 the former Spanish and Portuguese colonies had become independent nations."
unlabel sent
remove p
array yr = 1800 1810 1820 1830 1840 2026
ptr 小人 yr.6
say "小人该站哪儿？「变成独立国家」在哪一段？你来摆。"
open yr
ask tap yr "小人该站在哪一年？" expect=yr.4
学生: tap yr.4
