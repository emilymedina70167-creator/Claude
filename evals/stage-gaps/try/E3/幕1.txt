# E3 第 1 幕：一句只有一把谓语座位。laugh 的形态拨轮换成一张词形卡（学生点一格），座位和计数是贴着的字，金线银线、主语括号都要老师画
sentence s = "laugh probably has little influence on physical fitness."
paint s.1 s.3 g4
label seat s.3 "谓语座位（1 把）" side=above tone=key
label cnt s.8 "想当谓语的动词 2 · 座位 1" side=right tone=note
card f = "laugh 换成：" "laughs" "laughed" "laughing" "to laugh" "laughed（done）"
say "两个动词，一把座位。拨一拨 {s.1}，让这句话通顺。"
ask tap f "laugh 换成哪一个？"
学生: tap f.2
rewrite s "laughs probably has little influence on physical fitness."
pulse s.1 s.3
say "{s.1} 和 {s.3} 都想坐那把座位。"
ask tap f "再换一个试试"
学生: tap f.4
rewrite s "Laughing probably has little influence on physical fitness."
mark s.1 主语
retext cnt "想当谓语的动词 1 · 座位 1"
label zh s.1-8 "笑对身体健康的影响可能很小" side=below tone=soft
say "通了。它现在还是谓语吗？在句子里当什么？"
ask say "laughing 在句子里当什么？"
