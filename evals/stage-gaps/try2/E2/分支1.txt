# 时态拨盘：拨数驱动不了句子，只能学生点按钮、老师整句 rewrite；看 rewrite 以后灯（括号）、key 怎么样
sentence s = "If we are serious about ensuring that our science is both meaningful and reproducible, we must ensure that our institutions incentivise that kind of science." key="[Cl If we [V are] serious about [NF ensuring [Cl that our science [V is] both meaningful and reproducible]]], we [V must ensure] [Cl that our institutions [V incentivise] that kind of science]."
