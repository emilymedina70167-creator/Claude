# E1 第 2 幕（前半）：进行 = be + doing。拨时间、换主语，看 holding 一直不动；be 那格掏空让学生从 is / are 里挑。
# 时间轴：一排三格 + 格子下的指针当小人 + 一张贴的字当波浪（没有时间轴这种东西）。主语卷轴：一个数字拨数 + 图例。
# 拨数连不到句子，照第 1 幕的办法一种组合一个挑战（这里 4 种：过去、将来、I、I + 过去），每个只演一次。
# 空格：句子里没有空位的写法（＿＿ 会粘在前一个词上），拿原形 be 占着位置、上面标「?」；is / are 两块牌是另一句里的两个词，学生点一块（拖不进别的句子）。
sentence s = "Home prices are holding steady in most regions." key="Home prices [V are holding] steady in most regions."
mark s.3-4 谓语
array tl = 过去 现在 将来
ptr 小人 tl.2
label wave tl.2 "〰〰 正在进行" side=above tone=soft
dial t = 2 range=1-3 at=s
label tn dial:t "时间：1 过去 · 2 现在 · 3 将来" side=right
dial m = 1 range=1-2 at=s
label mn dial:m "主语：1 Home prices · 2 I" side=right
say "只拨时间、只换主语，看 {s.3-4} 里哪个词一直没动？"
goal p1 "时间拨到过去" when="t = 1, m = 1"
@p1 unmark s.1-5
@p1 rewrite s "Home prices were holding steady in most regions."
@p1 mark s.3-4 谓语
@p1 ptr 小人 tl.1
@p1 label wave tl.1 "〰〰 正在进行" side=above tone=soft
goal p3 "时间拨到将来" when="t = 3, m = 1"
@p3 unmark s.1-5
@p3 rewrite s "Home prices will be holding steady in most regions."
@p3 mark s.3-5 谓语
@p3 ptr 小人 tl.3
@p3 label wave tl.3 "〰〰 正在进行" side=above tone=soft
goal m2 "主语换成 I" when="t = 2, m = 2"
@m2 unmark s.1-5
@m2 rewrite s "I am holding steady in most regions."
@m2 mark s.2-3 谓语
@m2 refer s.2 -> s.1
goal m1 "I + 过去" when="t = 1, m = 2"
@m1 unmark s.1-5
@m1 rewrite s "I was holding steady in most regions."
@m1 mark s.2-3 谓语
@m1 refer s.2 -> s.1
@ask.right paint s.holding g4
ask tap s "哪个词一直没动？点它。" expect=s.holding
学生: dial t 1
学生: dial t 3
学生: dial t 2
学生: dial m 2
学生: dial t 1
学生: tap s.3
# 第二轮：老师把主语卷到 The price of these homes，be 掏空（原形 be 占位），两块牌让学生挑
unmark s.1-5
unrefer s.2
rewrite s "The price of these homes be holding steady in most regions."
mark s.6-7 谓语
tag s.6 ?
sentence c = "is are"
say "那前面那个词在管什么？这时候用哪个？先自己放。"
@ask.right rewrite s "The price of these homes is holding steady in most regions."
@ask.right refer s.6 -> s.2
@ask.right dim s.3-5
ask tap c "挑一块放进 {s.6} 那一格。" expect=c.1
学生: tap c.2
# 第三轮：学生挑了 are——照样放进去，线连到 homes、of 闪一下；of these homes 拉成一段让学生自己折起来看主语剩什么
rewrite s "The price of these homes are holding steady in most regions."
refer s.6 -> s.5
pulse s.3
mark s.3-5 修饰
open s fold
say "{s.6} 连到了 {s.5}。把 {s.3-5} 收起来，看主语剩下谁。"
@ask.right rewrite s "The price of these homes is holding steady in most regions."
@ask.right refer s.6 -> s.2
ask tap c "现在挑哪一块？" expect=c.1
学生: fold s.3-5
学生: tap c.1
