# E2 分支 3：从句挂错主人。学生从 that 的钩上拉线（open s.23 refer call=wrong），links= 写了两条常见错连，挂到 humans 页面说「就近挂到了 humans」并叫老师；
# 「代回去」的句子、单三和复数撞没撞、穿墙页面都不会：老师另造一句摆在下面。习题 7：which 挂到整句（松在句子外框上）
sentence s = "The most famous of these efforts was initiated by Noam Chomsky, who suggested that humans are born with an innate language-acquisition capacity that dictates a universal grammar." key="The most famous of these efforts [V was initiated] by Noam Chomsky, [Cl who [V suggested] [Cl that humans [V are born] with an innate language-acquisition capacity [Cl that [V dictates] a universal grammar]]]." links="s.23 -> s.22; !s.23 -> s.15=就近挂到了 humans; !s.23 -> s.11=挂到了 Chomsky，这一层被拍平了"
mark s.12-27 从句
mark s.14-27 从句
mark s.23-27 从句
open s.23 refer call=wrong
say "最后那个 {s.23} 说的是谁？从它的钩上拉线挂上去。"
goal g1 "把 that 的钩子挂对" when="s linked"
@g1 say "对，代回去：capacity dictates a universal grammar——读得通。"
学生: refer s.23 -> s.15
sentence back = "humans dictates a universal grammar."
glow back.1 back.2
say "代回去读：{back.1} {back.2}——撞了。"
学生: unrefer s.23
学生: refer s.23 -> s.22
remove back
remove s
