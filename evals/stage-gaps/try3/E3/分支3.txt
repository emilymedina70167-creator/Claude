# 代入句、逗号和隔着的词、句子主语、By 1830 两种挂法的直译：页面不给，老师写题卡；上面一句 a passing plane 也给一个钩对比
sentence b = "Passing the cafe, I saw a friend." key="[A Passing the cafe], [S I] [V saw] [O a friend]." links="b.1 -> b; !b.1 -> b.3=就近挂到了 cafe"
sentence a = "I saw a passing plane." links="a.4 -> a.5"
open b.1 refer call=wrong
open a.4 refer

goal g1 "两根线都连对" when="b linked, a linked"
@g1 say "对：挨着名词、没有逗号的修饰名词；隔着逗号的修饰整句。"
