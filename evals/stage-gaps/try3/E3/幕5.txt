# E3 第 5 幕：介词后只能接 doing；系动词后面的 to do 是表语。词组拖到卡后面、拨轮、翻卡、6×2 的表做不了：卡是两张题卡（老师翻），猜是打一行字；
# 话里指一张卡的一行写 {q.4}（看小块上是不是那一行的字）；三条真题句：学生自己框块、贴签（三句都 open mark），页面照 key 说「成分标错了」「断早了」，两种错一起；
# 等号对调：学生按住括号把表语那块拖到句首（open r move）、再把 is 挪到它后面；拼小卡：一排三格（To see、to believe 一格放不下两个词，只拼 Seeing is believing）
card q = "enjoy ＿＿" "want ＿＿" "begin ＿＿" "of ＿＿" "without ＿＿" "by ＿＿"
card b = "enjoy：doing 能 / to do 不能" "want：doing 不能 / to do 能" "begin：两个都能" "of：只能 doing" "without：只能 doing" "by：只能 doing"
cover b
say "先猜：每个词后面接 doing 还是 to do？"
ask say "六个词各接什么？按顺序写"
学生: say doing to do doing to do doing doing
uncover b
say "{q.4}、{q.5}、{q.6} 有什么共同点？"
ask say "介词后面接什么？"
学生: say 介词后面只能接 doing
remove q
remove b
sentence p = "Americans stopped taking prosperity for granted." key="[S Americans] [V stopped] [O taking prosperity for granted]."
sentence t = "Thinking is essentially a process of making connections in the brain." key="[S Thinking] [V is] essentially [P a process of [NF making connections in the brain]]."
sentence r = "The trick is to direct these funds better." key="[S The trick] [V is] [P to direct these funds better]."
open p mark undo=on
open t mark undo=on
open r mark undo=on
say "三句里的非谓语块框出来，点它当什么。"
ask next "框好了点下面" button="框好了"
学生: mark p.3-6 宾语
学生: mark t.1 主语
学生: mark t.7-9 非谓语
学生: mark r.4-7 宾语
学生: unmark r.4
学生: mark r.4-8 宾语
学生: next
open r move
say "{r.3} 后面这块是宾语吗？把它整块拖到句首，再把 {r.3} 挪到它后面，读一读。"
ask next "读完点下面" button="读通了"
学生: move r.4-8 before r.1
学生: move r.8 before r.6
学生: next
remove p
remove t
remove r
