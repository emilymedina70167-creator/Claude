# 课后测验标准答案（只给评卷人）

## p1（near）
题面：读下面的短文，回答问题，并给四个选项各说一句对或错的理由。

For decades, city planners treated street trees as decoration: pleasant to look at, but the first item to be cut when budgets grew tight. That view is changing. A growing body of research suggests that trees do measurable work. A mature tree can lower the surface temperature of the pavement beneath it by more than ten degrees, and neighbourhoods with dense canopies record fewer heat-related hospital visits during summer heatwaves.

Yet the benefits are unevenly shared. In many cities, wealthier districts have twice as much tree cover as poorer ones, largely because trees were planted where residents could afford to maintain them. Simply planting more saplings will not close that gap. Young trees need years of watering and care before they provide much shade, and nearly a third die within their first five years when no one is responsible for them.

Some cities have therefore begun to pay local residents to look after newly planted trees. Early results are encouraging: survival rates have risen, and residents report a stronger sense of ownership over their streets. Critics point out that such programmes are costly. Supporters reply that the true cost lies in doing nothing, since the price of a heatwave is paid in health, not in budgets.

The author mentions that nearly a third of young trees die within their first five years in order to show that
[A] putting trees in the ground is not enough by itself.
[B] planting more saplings will close the gap within five years.
[C] wealthier districts lose a third of their trees every five years.
[D] trees must grow for five years before they can be planted in streets.
答案：A。这句紧跟在 Simply planting more saplings will not close that gap 后面，是用来支撑它的：没人照看，种下的幼树三分之一活不过五年，所以光种下去不够。A 是这层意思的改写（planting saplings → putting trees in the ground，will not close the gap → not enough by itself），选项里没有原文的词。B 原词 planting more saplings、close the gap、five years 都在，但原文说的是种得再多也补不上差距，意思相反。C 原词 a third、five years 出现，但原文说的是没人照看的幼树，套到了富人区身上，对象偷换。D 原词 five years 出现，但原文是种下后头五年，偷换成种之前要长五年，关系偷换。
考的是：对每个错误选项说出错在哪：原词出现但对象 / 关系 / 说话人被偷换、无中生有、意思相反、推得过头（怎样算会：新题的四个选项，学生各说出一句对或错的理由，理由都对）

## p2（far）
题面：读下面的短文，回答问题，并给四个选项各说一句对或错的理由。

Public libraries were built to lend books, but a quiet experiment is widening that mission. In several towns, libraries now lend drills, sewing machines and even telescopes. Librarians say the point is not the objects themselves but the people they bring in: residents who had stopped visiting now come back for a drill and stay for a repair workshop. Borrowers add a practical argument. A household may use a drill for only a few minutes a year, so owning one makes little sense, and a shared tool stays out of the landfill. Whether such collections can last is less certain. Tools break far more often than books, and few library budgets were planned with repairs in mind.

According to the librarians, lending tools matters mainly because it
[A] puts the objects themselves at the centre of the library.
[B] helps the library win back people it had lost.
[C] teaches librarians how to repair what they own.
[D] keeps rarely used tools out of the landfill.
答案：B。原文：Librarians say the point is not the objects themselves but the people they bring in: residents who had stopped visiting now come back。B 是这句的改写（residents who had stopped visiting now come back → win back people it had lost），选项里没有原文的词。A 原词 the objects themselves 照搬，但原文说的是重点「不是」东西本身，意思相反。C 原词 repair 出现，但修理课是给来借工具的居民上的，不是教馆员，主体偷换。D 原文确实有「不进垃圾填埋场」这层意思，但那是借用者（Borrowers）的理由，题干问的是馆员（librarians）怎么说，说话的人被偷换。
考的是：读题干定位到原文那一句，按意思比选项：认出正确项是原文的同义改写，不靠选项里有没有原文的词（怎样算会：一篇没见过的短文的细节题，学生选对，并指出正确项改写的是原文哪一句）

## p3（far）
题面：读下面的短文，回答问题，并给四个选项各说一句对或错的理由。

Public libraries were built to lend books, but a quiet experiment is widening that mission. In several towns, libraries now lend drills, sewing machines and even telescopes. Librarians say the point is not the objects themselves but the people they bring in: residents who had stopped visiting now come back for a drill and stay for a repair workshop. Borrowers add a practical argument. A household may use a drill for only a few minutes a year, so owning one makes little sense, and a shared tool stays out of the landfill. Whether such collections can last is less certain. Tools break far more often than books, and few library budgets were planned with repairs in mind.

It can be inferred from the last two sentences that tool collections
[A] will be closed down within a few years.
[B] may be hard to keep up because repair costs were not budgeted for.
[C] break more often than books because they are used more.
[D] were planned carefully in library budgets.
答案：B。原文：Whether such collections can last is less certain. Tools break far more often than books, and few library budgets were planned with repairs in mind。工具常坏、预算里没算修理费，所以能不能维持下去不好说，B 对（few budgets were planned with repairs in mind → repair costs were not budgeted for）。A 推得过头：原文只说不确定，没说几年内会关。C 前半句 break more often than books 照搬原文，后面加的原因「用得多」原文没有，无中生有的因果。D 原词 planned、budgets 出现，但原文是很少有预算考虑到修理，意思相反。
考的是：对每个错误选项说出错在哪：原词出现但对象 / 关系 / 说话人被偷换、无中生有、意思相反、推得过头（怎样算会：新题的四个选项，学生各说出一句对或错的理由，理由都对）
