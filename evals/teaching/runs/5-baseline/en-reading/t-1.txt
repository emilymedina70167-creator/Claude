````board new id=b1 title="方法：回原文比意思" col=all
**短文**（考研英语一阅读的文风，三段）

For decades, city planners treated street trees as decoration: pleasant to look at, but the first item to be cut when budgets grew tight. That view is changing. A growing body of research suggests that trees do measurable work. A mature tree can lower the surface temperature of the pavement beneath it by more than ten degrees, and neighbourhoods with dense canopies record fewer heat-related hospital visits during summer heatwaves.

Yet the benefits are unevenly shared. In many cities, wealthier districts have twice as much tree cover as poorer ones, largely because trees were planted where residents could afford to maintain them. Simply planting more saplings will not close that gap. Young trees need years of watering and care before they provide much shade, and nearly a third die within their first five years when no one is responsible for them.

Some cities have therefore begun to pay local residents to look after newly planted trees. Early results are encouraging: survival rates have risen, and residents report a stronger sense of ownership over their streets. Critics point out that such programmes are costly. Supporters reply that the true cost lies in doing nothing, since the price of a heatwave is paid in health, not in budgets.

```gloss
decoration = 装饰品
budgets = 预算
measurable = 可测量的
mature = 成熟的；长成的
pavement = 人行道；路面
canopies = 树冠（层）
heatwaves = 热浪
unevenly = 不均匀地
districts = 城区
saplings = 树苗
maintain = 养护
shade = 树荫
survival = 存活
ownership = 主人翁感；归属感
critics = 批评者
```
````

````board add id=b2 title="重点：做选项题的三步" col=1
```key
title: 重点：做选项题的三步
1. **读题干，抓定位词**：段号、人名或群体名（critics、supporters）、关键名词。
2. **回原文找到那一句**（有时是相邻两三句），之后只拿这一句来比。
3. **逐个选项按意思比**：意思和那一句一样，留下；有一处意思变了，说出是哪一处，排除。
```

```remember
title: 要背
正确项：==换了词，意思不变==（同义改写，paraphrase）
错误项：==词常常照搬，意思变了==
```

$\chalk{blue}{\text{看到选项里的词和原文一模一样，不是加分，反而要多核对一遍意思。}}$
````

````board add id=b3 title="演示：一句话的同义改写" col=2
```frames
id: fr-para
title: 原文一句 → 正确项
---
**原文**（第 1 段）

A mature tree can lower the surface temperature of the pavement beneath it by more than ten degrees.

> 先看原文这一句：谁、做了什么、对谁、到什么程度。
---
**逐块换说法**

| 原文 | 改写 |
|---|---|
| a mature tree | a fully grown tree |
| lower the surface temperature of | cool |
| the pavement beneath it | the ground under it |
| by more than ten degrees | noticeably |

> 每一块换成同义的说法：主语、动作、对象、程度一样都没丢。
---
**改写成的正确项**

A fully grown tree can noticeably cool the ground under it.

> 词几乎全换了，意思一点没变：这就是正确项常见的样子。
---
**对照：词都在，意思却变了**

A mature tree can lower the temperature of a whole neighbourhood by more than ten degrees.

> mature tree、lower、temperature、ten degrees 全是原词，但降温的对象从「它底下的路面」换成了「整个街区」。这种造法下一块板专门讲。
```
````

````board plan
目标：学生回原文定位到那一句，对 Q1、Q2 的每个选项说出对/错的理由（同义改写 vs 原词偷换、无中生有、相反、过头）。
猜：学生容易被原词吸引（看到 twice、heatwave、budgets 就觉得有出处），分不清「词在原文」和「意思在原文」。
证实：第二块板用第 1 段自编选项让学生判是哪种造法、说出变的是哪一处；然后做 Q1，看会不会被 D（twice）吸引。
````
