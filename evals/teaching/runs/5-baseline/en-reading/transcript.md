For decades, city planners treated street trees as decoration: pleasant to look at, but the first item to be cut when budgets grew tight. That view is changing. A growing body of research suggests that trees do measurable work. A mature tree can lower the surface temperature of the pavement beneath it by more than ten degrees, and neighbourhoods with dense canopies record fewer heat-related hospital visits during summer heatwaves.

Yet the benefits are unevenly shared. In many cities, wealthier districts have twice as much tree cover as poorer ones, largely because trees were planted where residents could afford to maintain them. Some cities have therefore begun to pay local residents to look after newly planted trees. Early results are encouraging: survival rates have risen, and residents report a stronger sense of ownership over their streets. Critics point out that such programmes are costly. Supporters reply that the true cost lies in doing nothing, since the price of a heatwave is paid in health, not in budgets.

## 第 3 轮 · 老师

先说你那个习惯：用原词去原文里**找到那一句**，这样做没问题；但用「词一样」来**判断选项对不对**就不行了，因为出题人造错误项最常用的手法，恰恰就是把原词照搬过来、再把意思改掉。所以原词只能用来定位，判对错要看意思。这块板讲错误项的四种造法，最后有 6 道判断题，做完这 6 道就进入 Q1。

````board new id=b4 title="错误项的四种造法"

```key
title: 重点：错误项的四种造法（这节课的完整清单）
| 造法 | 长什么样 | 怎么查 |
|---|---|---|
| **偷换** | 原词几乎都在，但其中一样被换掉了：**对象**（说的是 A，换成了 B）、**关系**（因果倒过来、条件和结果对调、比较的两样换了）、**说话的人**（研究说的变成某个群体说的；批评者的话安到支持者头上） | 逐块对照原句：谁、做什么、对谁、谁说的 |
| **无中生有** | 选项里有一条原文根本没提到的信息：新的比较、新的原因、新的建议 | 回原句找这条信息，找不到就排除 |
| **意思相反** | 方向反了：多 ↔ 少，升 ↔ 降，有用 ↔ 没用，加了或去掉了 not | 看动词、比较词、否定词 |
| **推得过头** | 方向对，程度升了级：fewer → none，some → all，can → always，may → will | 看程度词、范围词 |
```

```remember
title: 要背

```
````

````board add id=b6 title="第 1 题：词对得上，错" col=1
| 原文 | 选项 |
|---|---|
| A growing body of research suggests … | City planners' own studies show … |
````

````board add id=b7 title="第 6 题：词对不上，对" col=2

|---|---|

| look after | care for |
| newly planted trees | young trees |
````

## 比意思块，不比词
这段要回答：词对得上的选项为什么会错，词对不上的为什么会对。

## 第 1 题：词对得上，错
| 原文 | 选项 |
|---|---|
| A growing body of research suggests … | City planners' own studies show … |
| trees do measurable work | trees do measurable work |

后半句一字不差，但**说这话的人**换了：原文里说「树有实际作用」的是研究；规划者几十年来恰恰把树当装饰品。这是偷换说话的人。

## 第 6 题：词对不上，对
| 原文 | 选项 |
|---|---|
| pay local residents | paying residents |
| look after | care for |
| newly planted trees | young trees |
| survival rates have risen | more of them survive |

每一块都能一一对上，意思一样，只是换了说法，所以是同义改写。无中生有说的是**原文里找不到这条信息**，不是**原文里找不到这几个词**。
