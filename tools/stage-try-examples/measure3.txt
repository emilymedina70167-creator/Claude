# 带括号的词：挪到前面再拉括号，事实里引成「(that) they …」
remove B
sentence p = "It hoped they would learn how lighting affected productivity. (that)" key="It [V hoped] [Cl they [V would learn] [Cl how lighting [V affected] productivity]. (that)]."
tally p V
open p mark
open p move
say "把 (that) 挪到 hoped 后面，再从它拉到这件事结束。"
ask next "好了点下面" button="好了"
学生: move p.10 after p.2
学生: mark p.3-10 从句
